About Emperor MEC. Foundation

Headquartered in Ogun State, Nigeria, the Emperor MEC. Foundation is dedicated to reviving and developing indigenous technology to drive national development across agriculture, education, and industry.

  • Vision: A Nigeria revitalised by homegrown technology.
  • Mission: To research, develop, and transfer appropriate technologies to local industries and communities.
