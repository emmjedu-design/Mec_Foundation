Project ID: R-2026-CB01 Status: Field Testing

CocoaBot.v2

Autonomous precision agricultural system optimized for high-density cocoa plantation management and pest identification.

01. Mission Objective

Cocoa farming in West Africa remains labor-intensive and susceptible to Black Pod Disease, which can decimate up to 40% of annual yields. The CocoaBot initiative was launched to engineer an autonomous solution capable of navigating rugged plantation terrain to perform three critical tasks: autonomous weeding, multispectral health monitoring, and precision fertilization.

"By integrating computer vision with indigenous mechanical engineering, we aim to increase net farm yield by 25% while reducing hazardous chemical exposure for farmers."

Fig 1.1: CocoaBot Propulsion & Navigation Schematic

02. Methodology

Terrain Navigation (SLAM)

Our researchers developed a custom Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) algorithm tuned for the irregular light patterns and dense undergrowth of cocoa groves. Unlike standard GPS-dependent systems, CocoaBot utilizes LiDAR-fused odometry to maintain centimeter-level accuracy under heavy canopy cover.

Deep Learning for Pod Analysis

The on-board compute module runs a proprietary Convolutional Neural Network (MEC-Vision) trained on over 50,000 images of cocoa pods at various stages of maturation and disease.
