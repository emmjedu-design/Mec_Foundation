CocoaBot v2 Completes 500-Hour Field Endurance Test in Ogun State

"The latest testing phase confirms 98.4% uptime and validates the autonomous navigation algorithm for irregular tropical canopies."

Figure 4.2: CocoaBot v2 during high-humidity night navigation trial.

The Emperor MEC. Foundation Engineering Team is pleased to announce the successful conclusion of the Phase 3 field trials for the CocoaBot v2 project. Over the past 500 operating hours, the system was subjected to various environmental stressors, including high-humidity precipitation and 35°C ambient temperatures typical of Western Nigeria.

Technical Progress

The primary focus of this dispatch is the validation of the MEC-Vision v4.1 navigation stack. Traditional GPS-based systems often fail under the thick foliage of cocoa groves. Our proprietary SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) variant utilized fused LiDAR and IMU data to maintain a sub-5cm positional accuracy throughout the trial.

Institutional Impact

This milestone represents a significant step toward the foundation’s goal of indigenous agricultural mechanization. By reducing the labor-intensive nature of weeding and pest identification, the CocoaBot system allows local farmers to scale their operations without increasing hazardous chemical exposure.

The foundation will now transition into Phase 4: Pilot Community Integration. This phase involves deploying three CocoaBot units to cooperative farms in Ogun State for real-world user feedback and operator training.
